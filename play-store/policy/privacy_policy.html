Privacy Policy for Gallerai: Photos, Video & AI

Effective date: 24 November 2026
App: Gallerai: Photos, Video & AI (Android, package com.photo_editor.my_photo_editor)
Developer: mydjearth
Contact: mydjearth@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gallerai ("the app", "we", "our") accesses, collects and uses, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos stay on your device. We do not upload, store or share them on any server.
  • All editing and AI features (AI Cutout, Copy text, smart search, face blur, auto enhance, clean up, document scan) run on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, which collects limited usage and device information.
  • The app has no ads, does not use the advertising ID, and does not sell any data.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

2. Permissions the app uses and why

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older)To show, organise, search, play and edit the photos and videos on your device. This is the core function of a gallery app.
Selected photos access (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, Android 14+)Lets you allow access to only the photos you choose, if you prefer.
Photo location (ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION)To show where a photo was taken in its details, and to remove that location when you use "Share without location". The location is read on your device only and is never sent anywhere.
Storage write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10 and older only)To save edited photos, PDFs, compressed images, GIFs and trimmed videos as new files on your device.
Internet and network stateUsed by Google Firebase (analytics and app configuration) and by Google Play services to download the on-device AI models once. Your photos are never uploaded.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Gallerai → Permissions.

3. Information processed on your device only

The following is processed locally and is never collected by us or sent to any server:

  • Photos and videos: viewing, editing, cutouts, filters, collages, PDFs, compression, video trimming and GIF creation all happen on your device. Edited results are saved as new files; your originals are not overwritten unless you choose to move, rename or delete them.
  • On-device AI analysis: to power smart search, "Things" albums and the Clean up tool, the app analyses your photos on your device (image labels, similarity and blur checks). These results are stored in a private database inside the app on your device.
  • Text recognition and face detection: Copy text and Blur faces analyse the photo on your device.
  • Photo location (EXIF GPS): read only to display it and to remove it when sharing.
  • Locked Folder: the list of items you hide is stored only inside the app on your device, protected by your device screen lock. Files are hidden inside the app; they are not uploaded or encrypted by the app.
  • Recycle Bin: uses the Android system Recycle Bin on your device (Android 11+).
  • Settings: your preferences (theme, accent colour, grid size and similar options) are saved only on your device.

4. Information we collect

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config, services provided by Google LLC, to understand how the app is used, fix problems and improve features. They collect:

  • App activity: which screens are opened and which features are used (for example, "editor opened").
  • App information and performance: basic diagnostics, such as app version and when the app is opened.
  • Device information and identifiers: device model, Android version, language, country (from the device settings or IP address) and a random app instance ID created by Firebase.

This information does not include your photos, videos, their contents, file names or location, and is not used to identify you personally. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or phone number through the app.

The AI features use Google Play services (ML Kit). The AI models are downloaded and run on your device. Google may receive basic, non-photo diagnostic information about the use of these services. Your photos are not sent to Google.

Learn how Google uses data from these services: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. How we use information

We use the information described in section 4 only to:

  • understand which features are useful and improve the app;
  • detect and fix crashes and performance problems;
  • deliver app configuration and update notices.

We do not use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of individual users.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade any information.

Information is shared only:

  • with Google (Firebase, Google Play services), as a service provider processing it on our behalf as described above;
  • when you choose to share a photo, video, PDF or text using Android's share menu. It then goes to the app you pick, under that app's privacy policy;
  • if required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device (analysis results, Locked Folder list, settings) is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for a limited period according to its data retention settings, and then deleted automatically.
  • To ask about or request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, contact us at mydjearth@gmail.com.

8. Security

Information sent to Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your photos and videos never leave your device through the app, which keeps them under your control.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/EEA, UK or India), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. You can:

  • revoke permissions at any time in your device settings;
  • clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove all data stored on your device;
  • contact us at mydjearth@gmail.com with any request.
