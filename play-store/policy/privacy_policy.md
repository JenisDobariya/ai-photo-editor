# Privacy Policy: Gallerai

_Last updated: [date of publishing]_

Gallerai ("the app") is a photo and video gallery with on-device editing and AI tools, published by [developer name] on Google Play. This policy explains what the app accesses and what data is collected.

## Photos and videos
- The app asks for permission to read the photos and videos on your device so it can show, organise and edit them. That is its core purpose as a gallery.
- **Your photos and videos are processed on your device and are never uploaded** to our servers or any cloud. AI features (cutout, copy text, smart search labels, face blur, enhance, cleaner, document scan) run on the device.
- Edited images are saved as new files on your device. Originals are not overwritten unless you choose to move, rename or delete them.

## Photo location
- If you allow it, the app reads the location stored inside a photo (EXIF GPS) to show it in the photo's details. This stays on your device.
- "Share without location" removes that data from the copy you share.

## Locked Folder
- Items you move to the Locked Folder are hidden inside the app behind your device screen lock. The list is stored only on your device. Files are not encrypted or uploaded.

## Data we collect
We use **Firebase Analytics and Remote Config** (Google) to understand how the app is used and to improve it. These collect:
- **App activity:** which screens and features are used.
- **Device information and identifiers:** device model, OS version, app version and an app instance identifier.
- **Diagnostics:** basic performance information.

The AI tools use **Google Play services (ML Kit)**. Google may receive basic diagnostic and usage information to deliver and improve these models. The photos themselves are not sent.

This data is sent encrypted (HTTPS), is not sold, and is not used for advertising. The app contains no ads and does not use the advertising ID.

Google's handling of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Your choices
- You can revoke photo, video or location permissions at any time in your device's Settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your device.
